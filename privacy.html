Privacy policy

Browser to Notion is operated by Owai. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Purpose and data used

The extension saves bookmarks from selected Chrome folders to the Notion database you authorize. It uses bookmark titles, URLs, folder paths, and bookmark dates. Its bookmarks permission lets it read your folder tree and determine which bookmarks are in scope. Only eligible bookmark details are sent for saving. It does not read website content or browsing history.

Storage and sharing

Your Chrome profile stores settings, selected folder IDs, a connection session, bookmark snapshots, and activity. Selected bookmark details pass through our hosted connection service on Supabase to Notion. The service stores encrypted Notion access and refresh tokens, workspace identifiers and names, hashed service credentials, short-lived authorization records, and save records containing hashed destination/website keys and Notion entry IDs and links. These records support authorization, retries, and duplicate prevention. Bookmark titles, source URLs, and folder paths are processed for requests and are not stored as plain text in the save ledger.

Supabase processes service traffic and operational metadata, such as IP addresses, request URLs, and timings. Notion processes the bookmark information sent to your selected database. The service temporarily uses an IP address or its hash to limit connection attempts for ten minutes. Providers apply their own privacy terms. The extension includes no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, or data sale features. Data is used to provide bookmark syncing.

Retention and deletion

Completed Chrome activity expires after 30 days when local activity is updated. Pending work remains until completed, cancelled, or you disconnect. Service credentials and save records expire after 90 days without activity and are removed by maintenance cleanup. Authorization records expire within ten minutes; temporary save and refresh locks expire within 90 seconds. If a free hosting project is paused, maintenance resumes after restoration.

Disconnecting through Settings removes active service records and extension data when the service is reachable. Saved Notion entries remain until you delete them there. Uninstalling alone does not notify the service to delete its records; inactive expiry or a deletion request still applies. No scheduled operator-managed database backups are configured for the initial free deployment. Infrastructure recovery copies and provider logs follow the hosting provider's own retention practices. Chrome/device backups and Notion retention are controlled separately.

Security and your controls

Hosted traffic uses HTTPS. Notion credentials are encrypted in the service database. Each installation has a separate connection session. You can select folders, pause syncing, clear completed activity, revoke access in Notion, and disconnect in Settings. Application code does not log bookmark bodies, OAuth codes, or tokens. Provider infrastructure logs are subject to provider policies.
